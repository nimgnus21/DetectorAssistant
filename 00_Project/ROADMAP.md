# ROADMAP

> DetectorAssistant Development Roadmap

Project: DetectorAssistant

Current Version: v1.0.0 (Draft)

Last Updated: 2026-08-07

---

# 1. Vision

DetectorAssistant aims to become a unified engineering knowledge platform for Flat Panel Detector (FPD) products.

The long-term objective is to provide standardized technical knowledge, engineering workflows, troubleshooting guidance, and AI-assisted retrieval for the complete detector lifecycle.

---
