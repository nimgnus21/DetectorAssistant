# Overview

This document defines the standard terminology related to X-ray generators used with Flat Panel Detectors (FPDs).

These terms are referenced throughout the DetectorAssistant knowledge base, including detector installation, exposure control, image acquisition, calibration, troubleshooting, and technical support.

This glossary focuses on terminology only. Generator operating principles and maintenance procedures should refer to the corresponding SOP or workflow documentation.

---

# X-ray Generator

## Definition

A device that generates X-rays by applying high voltage to an X-ray tube during an exposure.

## Primary Functions

- Generate X-rays
- Control exposure
- Provide trigger signals
- Synchronize with the detector

---

# Exposure

## Definition

The process during which the X-ray generator emits X-rays toward the detector.

## Related Terms

- Exposure Window
- Trigger
- Exposure Time

---

# Exposure Time

## Definition

The duration for which X-rays are emitted during a single exposure.

## Unit

- ms (Milliseconds)
- s (Seconds)

---

# Exposure Window

## Definition

The time interval during which the detector accepts X-ray exposure and performs image acquisition.

## Related Terms

- Evt_Exp_Enable
- Evt_Exp_Prohibit

---

# Trigger

## Definition

A control signal used to initiate detector image acquisition.

## Common Types

- Software Trigger
- Hardware Trigger
- AED Trigger

---

# Software Trigger

## Definition

An exposure trigger generated by software through the SDK.

## Typical Usage

- Detector testing
- Factory calibration
- SDK development

---

# Hardware Trigger

## Definition

An electrical trigger signal transmitted from the X-ray generator to the detector.

## Typical Usage

- Clinical imaging
- Industrial inspection

---

# AED (Automatic Exposure Detection)

## Definition

A detector operating mode that automatically detects X-ray exposure without requiring an external trigger signal.

## Characteristics

- No trigger cable required
- Exposure detected internally
- Simplified installation

---

# DR Trigger

## Definition

A synchronization signal exchanged between the detector and the X-ray generator for digital radiography systems.

---

# Exposure Synchronization

## Definition

The coordination of detector acquisition timing with X-ray generation to ensure correct image capture.

---

# Synchronization Mode

## Definition

The operating mode defining how the detector and generator synchronize image acquisition.

---

# Generator Ready

## Definition

The state indicating that the X-ray generator is prepared to perform an exposure.

---

# Exposure Ready

## Definition

The condition where both the detector and generator are ready for image acquisition.

---

# Exposure Enable

## Definition

A detector state allowing X-ray exposure to be accepted.

## Related SDK Event

- Evt_Exp_Enable

---

# Exposure Prohibit

## Definition

A detector state preventing image acquisition during X-ray exposure.

## Related SDK Event

- Evt_Exp_Prohibit

---

# kV (Kilovoltage)

## Definition

The peak voltage applied across the X-ray tube during exposure.

## Unit

kV

## Effect

- Determines X-ray energy
- Influences image penetration
- Affects image contrast

---

# mA (Milliampere)

## Definition

The tube current flowing through the X-ray tube during exposure.

## Unit

mA

## Effect

Determines X-ray output intensity.

---

# mAs (Milliampere-second)

## Definition

The product of tube current and exposure time.

## Formula

mAs = mA × Exposure Time

## Effect

Determines the total X-ray quantity produced during an exposure.

---

# Pulse Width

## Definition

The duration of a single X-ray pulse.

## Unit

Milliseconds (ms)

---

# Pulse Exposure

## Definition

An exposure mode in which X-rays are emitted as discrete pulses instead of continuously.

---

# Continuous Exposure

## Definition

An exposure mode where X-rays are emitted continuously throughout the exposure period.

---

# Dose

## Definition

The amount of X-ray radiation delivered during an exposure.

---

# Dose Level

## Definition

The relative amount of radiation used during image acquisition.

## Typical Usage

- Low Dose
- Medium Dose
- High Dose

---

# SID (Source-to-Image Distance)

## Definition

The distance between the X-ray focal spot and the detector imaging surface.

## Unit

mm

cm

---

# Generator Interface

## Definition

The physical or electrical interface connecting the X-ray generator to the detector.

---

# Trigger Cable

## Definition

A cable used to transmit trigger signals between the generator and detector.

---

# Exposure Delay

## Definition

The time between receiving a trigger signal and the start of X-ray generation.

---

# Exposure Timeout

## Definition

A condition where the expected X-ray exposure does not occur within the configured waiting period.

---

# Image Acquisition Timeout

## Definition

A condition where the detector waits for an exposure but does not receive image data within the expected time.

## Related SDK Event

- Evt_WaitImage_Timeout

---

# Generator Warm-up

## Definition

The procedure performed before imaging to stabilize the X-ray tube and generator.

---

# Generator Communication

## Definition

The exchange of synchronization or control signals between the detector and generator.
