# 1. Purpose

This document defines how internal training materials are referenced within the DetectorAssistant knowledge base.

Training materials are considered authoritative internal engineering references and provide standardized explanations of detector principles, workflows, and troubleshooting methods.

This document does not duplicate training content.

Instead, it establishes traceability between training materials and knowledge-base documents.

---

# 2. Scope

Training materials may include:

- Product Training
- FAE Training
- Hardware Training
- Software Training
- SDK Training
- Calibration Training
- Image Quality Training
- Troubleshooting Training
- Customer Support Training

# 5. Usage Rules

Training materials should be used to:

- Explain engineering principles.
- Standardize workflows.
- Clarify operating procedures.
- Support troubleshooting logic.

Training materials should not be copied directly into the knowledge base.

Engineering interpretation and field experience should be added where appropriate.

---

# 6. Maintenance Rules

Update this reference when:

- New training materials are released.
- Existing training content is revised.
- Engineering best practices change.
